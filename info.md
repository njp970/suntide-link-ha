# Suntide Link

Inverter + battery data at **1-second resolution from your LAN**, plus
optional cloud intelligence: the day's plan, a written story of your
battery's day, monthly earnings, and a `grid_event_active` binary sensor
your automations can react to.

- **Local never depends on cloud** — cancel Suntide and your sensors keep
  running.
- Zeroconf discovery: the Link appears by itself; nothing to type.
- Cloud token optional, read-only, revocable in the Suntide app.
